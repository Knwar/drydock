# Capture: Keep the User's Voice

Adapted from the capture skill in alirezarezvani/claude-skills (MIT).

## The core rule

If the user said it casually, restate it casually. Notes are for them, not for an imagined corporate reader. Restating a casual idea in corporate language loses the energy that made it worth capturing.

## Side-by-side

| User said | Corporate-ified (don't) | Voice kept (do) |
|---|---|---|
| "build something crazy with AI" | "Explore innovative AI-driven solutions" | "Build something crazy with AI" |
| "figure out the damn pricing already" | "Conduct comprehensive pricing strategy analysis" | "Figure out pricing (final answer)" |
| "make the landing page not suck" | "Optimize landing page user experience metrics" | "Make the landing page not suck" |
| "talk to Sarah about the thing" | "Schedule alignment discussion with Sarah re: outstanding initiative" | "Talk to Sarah about the thing" |
| "I'm tired of debugging this" | "Investigate root causes of recurring debugging friction" | "Tired of debugging this: find the root cause" |

## What counts as voice

- **Register**: formal or casual, dry or energetic
- **Vocabulary**: the user's exact nouns ("Sarah", "the thing"), not "stakeholder" or "initiative"
- **Cadence**: short phrases stay short
- **Slang and profanity**: keep as-is
- **Self-talk**: "ugh", "actually", "ok so" signal real thinking and belong in the note

## What may change

- Punctuation and typos
- Imperative form in the action list: "I should email Sarah" becomes "Email Sarah"
- Light disambiguation: if "the thing" is genuinely unclear, note it and ask; don't replace it silently

## What never happens

- Replacing the user's nouns with "platform", "solution", "initiative", "framework"
- Adding qualifiers they didn't say: "explore", "leverage", "deep dive into"
- "Implement", "Establish", "Facilitate" prefixes for no reason
- Removing emotion: "I'm pissed about X" becoming "There is a concern regarding X"
- Dropping an item because it seemed trivial

## Project names

Cluster names use the user's words. "Q3 launch", "Q3 pricing", "Q3 emails" is "Q3 Launch", not "Q3 Go-to-Market Initiative".

## The test

Before writing each line: would the user recognise this as something they'd say? If not, rewrite it.
