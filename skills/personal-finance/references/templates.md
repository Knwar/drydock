# Personal Finance Templates

## Default categories

Use the user's own if they have them. Otherwise start here and merge or rename freely.

| Category | Type | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| Housing | Fixed | Rent or mortgage, service charge, property tax |
| Utilities | Variable essential | Energy, water, internet, phone |
| Groceries | Variable essential | Supermarkets, food shops |
| Transport | Variable essential | Fuel, public transport, parking, car costs |
| Insurance | Fixed | Home, car, health, life |
| Debt repayments | Fixed | Loans, minimum card payments (not card purchases) |
| Health | Variable essential | Prescriptions, dentist, gym if essential to the user |
| Subscriptions | Discretionary | Streaming, software, memberships |
| Eating out | Discretionary | Restaurants, takeaway, coffee |
| Shopping | Discretionary | Clothes, household, electronics |
| Entertainment and travel | Discretionary | Events, hobbies, holidays |
| Gifts and giving | Discretionary | Presents, donations |
| Fees and interest | Variable | Bank fees, overdraft, card interest |
| Savings and sinking funds | Transfer | Moves to savings (track, but not as spend) |
| Uncategorised | — | Must be empty by the end |

## Anomaly checklist

- [ ] Duplicates: same merchant + same amount within a few days (confirmed not two real purchases)
- [ ] Recurring charges above their usual amount (old vs new, annual difference)
- [ ] New or unrecognised merchants, especially small or foreign charges
- [ ] Free trials that converted to paid
- [ ] Fees and interest charged
- [ ] Expected items missing (income, a regular bill)
- [ ] Categories more than 50% above their 3-month average

Suspicious charge: the user contacts their bank through the number on their card or the bank's official app, never through a link or number in a message.
