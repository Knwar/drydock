# Show notes and episode templates

## Show notes

```markdown
# <Episode number if used>: <Title>

<One to two sentences: who, what, why it matters to the listener. This is what previews show.>

<Two to four sentences or bullets on what's covered.>

## In this episode
- <Key point as a claim, not a topic> (<timestamp if in transcript>)
- ...

## Chapters
00:00 <label>
<mm:ss> <label>
...
<Or: "No chapters: transcript has no timestamps." / "Timings from the unedited recording; re-time after the edit.">

## Guest
<Name>, <role/credential exactly as supplied>. <One or two sentences from the supplied bio or what they said in the episode.>
Where to find them: <supplied links, or [NEEDS LINK]>

## Mentioned in this episode
- <Resource>: <what was said about it> (<timestamp>) <URL or [NEEDS LINK]>
- ...

## Quote
"<Exact line from the transcript>" (<speaker>, <timestamp>)

## Credits
Host: <name> · Produced by: <name> · Music: <credit as supplied>
<Sponsor disclosure, if any>
<Show's standard footer: subscribe, newsletter, contact>
```

## Episode script / run-of-show

```markdown
# <Show> — Ep <n>: <working title>
Target runtime: <mm> · Format: <solo / co-host / interview> · Record date: <date>

Central question / promise: <one sentence>

## Cold open (0:00–0:45)
<Scripted line or the clip to use (from a previous recording: in/out times)>

## Intro (0:45–1:30)
[SCRIPTED] <Show name, host(s), the episode promise in one sentence, guest one-liner>

## Segment 1: <name> (1:30–<mm:ss>)
Purpose: <what this segment does>
Transition in: <line>
- <Talking point or question>
- <Follow-up prompt>
Story/example to use: <...>

## Segment 2: <name> (...)
...

## Mid-roll (<mm:ss>) [if any]
[SCRIPTED] <Sponsor read with disclosure>

## Segment 3: <name> (...)
...

## Outro (<mm:ss>–end)
[SCRIPTED] <The one takeaway> · <One call to action> · <Next episode tease> · <Credits>

## Production notes
Music cues: · Clips to pull: · Links promised on air (collect for notes):
```

Mark lines `[SCRIPTED]` to read word for word; everything else is talking points.
